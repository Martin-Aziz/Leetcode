## 3. 🎯 DOMAIN MODEL

### What Does This App Do?
This is a **LeetCode interview coaching knowledge base** that provides structured learning materials for coding interview preparation. It contains 22+ algorithm problems, each with two learning modes: (1) **Teaching Mode** with progressive hints, detailed explanations, and step-by-step walkthroughs, and (2) **Mock Interview Mode** with timed challenges, scoring rubrics, and evaluation criteria.

### Who Uses It?
**Primary Users:** Software engineers preparing for technical interviews at FAANG/top tech companies, computer science students building algorithm skills, and self-taught developers seeking structured practice. **Secondary Users:** Coding bootcamp instructors, technical interview coaches, and study group facilitators.

### Core Value Proposition
Delivers **quality coaching over quantity** by teaching reusable algorithm patterns (Hash Map, Sliding Window, DFS/BFS, Dynamic Programming, etc.) rather than memorizing individual solutions, enabling users to recognize and solve 80% of interview problems through pattern mastery.
